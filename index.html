🛡️ Expert Dietary Strategy

Navigating New York City on a strict Low-FODMAP (Monash compliant), Gluten-Free (GF), and Dairy-Free (DF) diet requires precision. The highest risks in restaurant settings are hidden fructans (onions, garlic) used in stocks, sauces, and marinades, alongside cross-contamination with gluten and dairy.

🚫 The "No" List

  • Allium: Onion, garlic, shallots, leeks (even powders).
  • Gluten: Wheat, barley, rye, regular soy sauce.
  • Dairy: Milk, butter, cream, most cheeses.

✅ The "Safe" List

  • Proteins: Plain grilled meats, poultry, fish, eggs.
  • Carbs: Rice, potatoes, quinoa, GF tamari.
  • Fats: Olive oil, canola oil (check for garlic infusion).

🗣️ Ordering Tactics

  • Always state it is a severe allergy to ensure the kitchen takes it seriously.
  • Ask for food cooked in clean pans with plain olive oil.
  • Avoid complex sauces; stick to lemon/salt/pepper.
