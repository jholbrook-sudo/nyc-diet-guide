🛡️ Expert Dietary Strategy

Navigating New York City on a strict Low-FODMAP (Monash compliant), Gluten-Free (GF), and Dairy-Free (DF) diet requires precision. The highest risks in restaurant settings are hidden fructans (onions, garlic) used in stocks, sauces, and marinades, alongside cross-contamination with gluten and dairy.

🚫 The "No" List

  • Allium: Onion, garlic, shallots, leeks (even powders).
  • Gluten: Wheat, barley, rye, regular soy sauce.
  • Dairy: Milk, butter, cream, most cheeses.

✅ The "Safe" List

  • Proteins: Plain grilled meats, poultry, fish, eggs.
  • Carbs: Rice, potatoes, quinoa, GF tamari.
  • Fats: Olive oil, canola oil (check for garlic infusion).

🗣️ Ordering Tactics

  • Always state it is a severe allergy to ensure the kitchen takes it seriously.
  • Ask for food cooked in clean pans with plain olive oil.
  • Avoid complex sauces; stick to lemon/salt/pepper.

🚶 Penn Station to Central Park Route

Walking from Penn Station (34th St & 8th Ave) up to Central Park (59th St) is a vibrant ~1.2 mile walk straight up 8th Avenue. Since Kelvin is arriving later in the evening, here are some notes on eating safely along this corridor:

🍕 The "By-The-Slice" Pizza Reality

Severe Allergy Warning: Getting GF/DF/Low FODMAP pizza "by the slice" from a typical NYC window is virtually impossible due to severe flour cross-contamination and pre-made garlic/onion heavy sauces. Instead, go to a place like Don Antonio (on your route) and order a personal pie. Ask for a "white pizza" (no tomato sauce, no cheese) with safe olive oil, prosciutto, and arugula to ensure it is 100% safe.

🥨 NYC Street Food Safety

Avoid Halal carts, hot dog stands, and pretzel carts (cross-contamination, garlic/onion marinades, gluten). Safe Street Food: Look for Fresh Fruit Carts (grab bananas or oranges) or Nuts4Nuts carts. Stick to plain roasted peanuts or macadamia nuts (avoid cashews/pistachios due to high FODMAPs, and avoid honey-roasted).

🎭 Broadway Box Office Strategy

Since you and your dad plan to walk to the box offices to secure the best prices for your shows, here is your logistical battle plan from your hotel at 11 W 37th St. Going directly to the box office is a great way to avoid online ticketing fees!

🧙‍♀️ Wicked

Gershwin Theatre

📍 222 W 51st St (approx 0.9 miles walk)

Walk directly up Broadway/7th Ave. Ask the box office agent if they have any partial view, standing room, or rush options available for the dates you are there.

🍰 Two Strangers...

Longacre Theatre

📍 220 W 48th St (approx 0.7 miles walk)

A shorter walk, just off Broadway. Because this is a newer musical transfer, your odds of securing excellent box office pricing are very high. Go right when the box office opens (usually 10 AM).

🎟️ TKTS Times Square

Under the Red Steps

📍 Broadway & 47th St (approx 0.6 miles walk)

If the individual box offices don't have the prices you want, check the TKTS booth for 20-50% off same-day tickets. (Wicked is rarely on TKTS, but "Two Strangers" often is!).
